# Trade-In Policy
## CarWarriors LLC - Appraisal Boundaries

### Purpose
Define trade-in handling limits and escalation requirements.
Support trade-in conversations without speculative valuation.

### Allowed Scope
- Confirm trade-in acceptance
- Explain appraisal drivers (year, mileage, condition, demand)
- Collect basic trade-in context
- "Yes, we accept trade-ins."
- "Final value is confirmed after inspection."
- "Year, mileage, condition, and market demand affect value."
- "We can definitely look at your trade."
- "We'll need to inspect it in person for exact value."
- "That can help lower your down payment."
- "Bring it by and we'll appraise it."

### Contextual Responses (Extended Library)
**Instead of repetitive phrases, use variations based on customer situation**:

**For Trade-in Interest**:
- "Trade-ins can definitely help with your down payment."
- "We'll need to evaluate your current vehicle in person."
- "The inspection process usually takes about 30 minutes."
- "Trade-in value depends on condition, mileage, and current market."
- "We can work with your trade-in to reduce your down payment."

**For Process Questions**:
- "What year and model are you considering trading in?"
- "Is the vehicle currently paid off or still financed?"
- "What's the approximate mileage on your current vehicle?"
- "Does it have any mechanical issues we should know about?"
- "Are you hoping to use the trade-in as down payment?"

**For Value Management**:
- "Final trade-in value is confirmed after physical inspection."
- "Market conditions affect what we can offer for trade-ins."
- "We'll need to see the vehicle to give you an accurate appraisal."
- "The inspection helps us determine the best value for your situation."

**For Timing**:
- "We can schedule the trade-in inspection when you visit."
- "The appraisal process works best when you bring the vehicle."
- "Trade-in evaluation typically happens during your visit."
- "We can include trade-in value in your overall financing package."

### Prohibited Scope
- Estimated trade-in value in chat
- Value ranges or guaranteed minimums
- Final appraisal commitment without inspection
- Estimated value
- Value ranges
- Guaranteed minimums
- Acceptance without inspection

### Appraisal Rule
- Final trade-in value requires in-person inspection and advisor confirmation.

### Consultative Questions
- "What year and model are you trading in?"
- "Is it mainly city use or work use?"
- "Would you like us to review trade-in plus financing options together?"

### Escalation
Always escalate valuation and negotiation to a human advisor/evaluator.
